# Stillwander

A wander and a sword through seven feudal-Japan-inspired lands. Single-file WebGL game (three.js). Plays from the prologue to the end of Chapter Two.

Open `index.html`, or play it on GitHub Pages.

Controls: click or hold to walk (or W A S D), F to slash (hold for a heavy strike; cuts trees, bamboo and shrubs for wood, bamboo and straw), hold R to guard, H to heal with Resolve, B to rest at a campfire, C to craft the bow and arrows, hold G or right-click to draw and release to loose, 1 / 2 for Reed and Stone stance, T to arm a blessing, Space to hum, Q / E to turn, scroll to zoom, M for sound.
